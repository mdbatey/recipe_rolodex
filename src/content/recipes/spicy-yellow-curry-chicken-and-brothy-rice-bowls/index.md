## Ingredients

### Marinated chicken

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2-inch piece ginger, peeled and grated
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- Zest of 1 lime
- Juice of ½ lime
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce (or tamari)
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 2 tablespoons sriracha

### Yellow curry broth

- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 3 tablespoons yellow curry paste (start with 2 for milder)
- 1 to 2 large shallots, peeled and quartered
- 5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 4-inch piece ginger, sliced into coins
- 4 cups chicken stock
- 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- Lime juice, to taste

### To serve

- 2 cups cooked jasmine rice
- Crispy shallots (below), fresh mint, lime wedges, chili oil

### Crispy shallots (optional)

- 2 large shallots, thinly sliced
- ½ cup olive oil
- Kosher salt

## Steps

1. Whisk the marinade ingredients in a large bowl, add the chicken, and toss. Let it sit at least 15 minutes while you start the broth.
2. Melt the coconut oil in a large pot over medium heat and fry the curry paste for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring. Add the shallots, garlic and ginger, pour in the stock, and simmer gently for 20 to 25 minutes.
3. If making crispy shallots, cover the sliced shallots with the olive oil in a small saucepan and cook over medium, then medium-low once the bubbling settles, about 20 minutes until golden. Drain on paper towel and salt well; they crisp as they cool. Save the oil.
4. Drain the chicken and sear it in a skillet over medium heat, about 5 minutes on the first side and 3 on the second, until charred and 165°F inside. Rest, then slice.
5. Strain the aromatics out of the broth. Stir in the coconut milk and warm 1 to 2 minutes over medium-low, then add the fish sauce and brown sugar. Take it off the heat and add lime juice to taste.
6. Divide the rice among bowls, top with the chicken, and ladle the broth over. Finish with crispy shallots, mint, lime and chili oil.
7. Store chicken, broth and rice separately for 3 to 4 days, or freeze the chicken and broth up to 3 months.
