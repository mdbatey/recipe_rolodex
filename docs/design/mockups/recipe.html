Grandma’s Banana Bread

added Oct 2026

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 4x8 loaf pan.
  2. Mash the bananas in a big bowl, then stir in the melted butter.
  3. Mix in the baking soda and salt, then the sugar, egg and vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour until just combined. Lumps are fine.
  5. Pour into the pan and bake about 1 hour, until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack.

Note: walnuts if you like, never raisins. — G.
