## Ingredients

### Salmon

- 1 pound salmon, skin removed, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 tablespoon avocado oil
- 1 teaspoon lime zest
- ½ lime, juiced
- 1 ½ tablespoons brown sugar (or coconut sugar)
- ¾ teaspoon chipotle chili powder
- ¾ teaspoon cumin
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon paprika
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

### Corn avocado peach salsa

- 1 medium peach, ripe but still slightly firm, diced
- 1 large avocado, ripe but still slightly firm, diced
- 1 large ear of corn, kernels cut off (about ¾ cup)
- 2 tablespoons finely diced red onion
- ½ jalapeño, seeded and diced (slice the other half for topping)
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped cilantro
- ½ small lime, juiced
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Sauce

- Spicy cilantro yogurt sauce (a separate recipe on Ambitious Kitchen)

### To serve

- 8 soft corn tortillas
- Thinly sliced jalapeño
- Shredded red cabbage
- Extra cilantro

## Steps

1. Make the cilantro yogurt sauce and keep it in the fridge until serving.
2. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
3. In a bowl, toss the salmon cubes with the oil, lime zest and juice, brown sugar, spices, salt and pepper until evenly coated. Spread them out on the baking sheet and bake 15 to 20 minutes.
4. While the salmon bakes, stir all the salsa ingredients together in a bowl, cover, and chill.
5. Char the tortillas if you like. Fill each with salmon, cabbage and salsa, then top with sliced jalapeño, a drizzle of yogurt sauce and more cilantro. Two tacos per person.
