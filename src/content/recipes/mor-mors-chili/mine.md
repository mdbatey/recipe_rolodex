## Ingredients

- 4 cups red kidney beans (two 16-ounce cans)
- 2 pounds ground turkey
- 2 potatoes, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced (3 teaspoons)
- 1/4 cup oil
- 2 cups canned tomatoes (one 16-ounce can)
- 3 tablespoons chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper
- 3/4 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Steps

1. Sauté the garlic and bell pepper in the oil until soft.
2. Add the turkey and brown it, then spoon off any grease.
3. Add the potatoes and the rest of the ingredients and simmer for 30 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
