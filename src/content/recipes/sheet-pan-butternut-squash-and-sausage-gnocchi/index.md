## Ingredients

### Sheet pan

- 1 (16 oz) package shelf-stable potato gnocchi
- 1 lb butternut squash (about 3 ½ cups), peeled and cubed small
- 1 medium red onion, cut into thick slices
- 3 to 4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons grated Parmesan
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 1 lb uncooked turkey or chicken sausage, casings removed
- 4 to 5 cups packed curly kale, stems removed, torn

### Miso-cider sauce

- 2 tablespoons white miso paste (or tahini)
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 2 teaspoons maple syrup
- ¼ teaspoon cracked black pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. In a large bowl, toss the gnocchi (no need to boil it), squash, onion, olive oil, Parmesan, thyme, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
2. Spread it in an even layer on a large rimmed baking sheet. Pinch the sausage into gnocchi-sized pieces and tuck them in between.
3. Roast 25 minutes, tossing once at 15 minutes.
4. In the same bowl, massage the kale with a little olive oil and a pinch of salt for about 15 seconds, until it softens.
5. For the sauce, mash the miso with a splash of hot water until smooth, then whisk in the oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup and pepper. If using tahini, whisk everything together and thin with 1 to 2 tablespoons of water.
6. Toss the kale onto the pan and roast 5 more minutes, until wilted.
7. Drizzle with the sauce and serve. Leftovers keep 3 days; reheat in a covered skillet with a splash of water.
