## Ingredients

- 4 cups red kidney beans (two 16-ounce cans)
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 2 onions, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced (3 teaspoons)
- 1/4 cup oil
- 2 cups canned tomatoes (one 16-ounce can)
- 4 tablespoons chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper
- 3/4 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Steps

1. Sauté the onion and garlic in the oil until translucent.
2. Add the beef and brown it, then spoon off the grease.
3. Add the rest of the ingredients and simmer for 30 minutes.
