## Ingredients

### Broth

- 1 whole chicken
- 1 carrot, quartered
- 2 celery sticks, halved
- 1 brown onion, quartered
- 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
- 2 litres water
- Salt, generously

### Soup

- 400 g long-grain rice, rinsed
- 3 eggs
- Juice of 3 lemons
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve

- Fresh dill
- Extra virgin olive oil

## Steps

1. Put the chicken, carrot, celery, onion and coriander seeds in a large pot with the water. Salt it well; the broth needs more than you'd think. Cover, bring to a boil, then hold at a steady simmer for about 40 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
2. Lift out the chicken to cool. Strain the broth into a clean pot and throw away the vegetables and seeds.
3. Add the rinsed rice to the broth and simmer until it's nearly tender.
4. Meanwhile, whisk the eggs with the lemon juice in a bowl. Whisk in 6 to 8 ladles of the hot broth, one at a time, so the eggs warm up without curdling.
5. Pour the egg-lemon mixture into the rice pot and heat gently for 3 to 4 minutes. Don't let it boil.
6. Shred the chicken off the bone and stir it in. Season with salt and pepper, then finish each bowl with dill and a drizzle of olive oil.
